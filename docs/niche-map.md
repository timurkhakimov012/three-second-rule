# Niche map

Which openings and formats work **now** in a niche, and which have stopped working.

## What you say

> what works in home-espresso TikTok right now? · map these five creators · what works for my account?

## How it runs

1. **Creators.** 5–10 creators who post in the niche, of different sizes (you name them, or the agent asks for seeds;
   handles are never guessed). Each one's last 25 posts are read.
2. **Rank.** Every post of the last 30 days against **its own creator's usual** (the median of that creator's other
   settled posts from the window and the 30 days before, at least 8). A post at 2× or more is a trend. A small
   creator's 5× outranks a big creator's ordinary post.
3. **Watch.** The top trends (two per creator at most), three ordinary posts and two flops are downloaded and measured,
   and the agent looks at their first three seconds.
4. **Formats.** The agent groups posts into formats — a repeatable container of hook, structure, look and length, not
   a topic.
5. **Map.** Each format is measured and placed.

## The map

| Corner | Where | Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Scale it** | used by ≥ 40% of the creators, lift ≥ 1 | widely used and working: make it now |
| **Underused** | fewer creators, lift ≥ 1 | works where it is used: an opening |
| **Overused** | common, lift < 1 | everyone does it, it no longer lifts |
| **Drop** | rare, lift < 1 | skip it |

- **lift** = the median of each creator's median ratio in that format. Every creator counts once, so one prolific
  creator cannot carry a format.
- **direction** — rising, steady or fading — compares the later half of the window with the earlier half. It is shown
  beside the corner, never mixed into it: a Scale-it format can be fading, and the answer says so.
- **3 creators or more** make a format of the niche. With one it is that creator's habit; with two, "few creators".
  Two can be one copying the other.

## One account

"What works for me?" runs the same way with one creator: the formats are compared against the account's own usual,
and "common" means 15% of its posts.

## When there is not enough

Under 3 creators with their own usual, or under 20 scored posts: the trends are listed, no format of the niche is
called, and the agent offers to read more.
